What is the Embedding Layer (W_E)?

Neural networks cannot perform math on words or discrete integers (like ID 6864). The embedding layer acts as a massive lookup dictionary. It contains 50,304 rows (one for every possible token) and 2048 columns. When the model receives a token ID, it literally extracts that specific row from the matrix. Through backpropagation during training, the model adjusts these numbers so that words with similar meanings drift closer together in this 2048-dimensional hyper-space.
