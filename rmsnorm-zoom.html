MICRO-MATH // RMS NORM
Root Mean Square Normalization
yi = xi / √( 1/n ∑x² + ε ) × γi
RMS = 0.00
Raw activations from previous layer. High variance causes mathematical instability.
Why RMSNorm?
Standard LayerNorm centers the mean to 0. RMSNorm drops mean-centering entirely. It is computationally cheaper and equally effective.
Epsilon (ε)
OLMoE uses ε = 1e-5 to prevent division by zero in case the Root Mean Square approaches exactly zero.
Learned Weight (γ)
Shape: [2048]. After forcing the vector into a strict range, the network applies a learned scale parameter (gamma) to restore expressiveness.
