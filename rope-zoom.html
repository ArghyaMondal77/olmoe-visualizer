Relative Distance
Because position is injected as an angle, the difference between token 2 and 4 is the exact same angle difference as token 12 and 14. This allows the model to easily calculate distance.
Frequency Decay
Notice how DIM [0,1] spins wildly, but DIM [4,5] barely moves? The rotation frequency exponentially decays across the 2048 dimensions. This captures both local precision and global context.
Infinite Context
Unlike Absolute Position embeddings (which have a hard cap, e.g., 2048 max tokens), RoPE can mathematically extrapolate beyond its training length because you can always rotate a circle further.
