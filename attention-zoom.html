Step 1: The Raw Dot Product. Multiplying the Query and Key matrices yields huge, high-variance numbers. Row 1 ("capital") strongly resonates with Col 3 ("France").

THE NUMERATOR Q . K^T & TEMPERATURE

Every word (Query) asks every other word (Key): "How relevant are you to me?" Because OLMoE operates in 128 dimensions per head, the dot product produces massively large numbers.

We divide by √128 (approx 11.3). This Temperature Scaling squashes the variance down to a stable range to prevent the Softmax from peaking at 99.99% and killing gradients.

THE CAUSAL MASK ( M)

Decoder-only models generate text autoregressively. During parallel training, a word cannot be allowed to look at words that come after it.

We enforce this by adding -∞ to the upper triangle of the matrix. The Softmax translates e^-∞ to exactly 0.000%, guaranteeing future words are completely ignored.

THE VALUE EXTRACTION (V)

The resulting probabilities are multiplied by the Value V matrix. If "capital" pays 85% of its attention to "France", it absorbs 85% of the semantic meaning from the "France" Value vector.

This process happens simultaneously across all 16 heads. Each head extracts a different 128-dimensional perspective (e.g., Head 0 extracts grammar, Head 1 extracts geography).

CONCATENATION & W_O PROJECTION

After all 16 heads compute their 128-dimensional Value outputs, they are concatenated back together side-by-side 16 times 128 = 2048.

Finally, this rejoined 2048D vector is multiplied by the Output Projection Matrix W_O. This matrix mixes the insights from all 16 isolated heads back together, producing the final attention payload that will be added to the residual spine.
