Step 0: The token arrives as a single 2048-dimensional vector from the LayerNorm.

THE COCKTAIL PARTY (Q, K, V)

Think of Attention like a cocktail party.

Query (Q): What I am looking for (e.g., "I need a verb to attach to").

Key (K): What I have to offer (e.g., "I am an action verb").

Value (V): What I actually mean (My underlying semantic payload).

When Q and K multiply, they create an affinity score. High scores mean V is passed along.

WHY MULTI-HEAD?

If the model only had one massive 2048D head, it could only focus on one type of relationship at a time.

By shattering the vector into 16 separate heads, the network can process 16 different perspectives simultaneously. Head 0 might track pronoun references (He -> John), Head 1 might track grammar, and Head 2 might track geographical entities.

THE DIMENSIONAL MATH

OLMoE-1B-7B has a model dimension of 2048.

It uses 16 attention heads.

Therefore, the dimension of each head is exactly 2048 / 16 = 128.

This slicing requires no extra parameters. It is purely a reshape operation: [1, 2048] ➔ [1, 16, 128].
