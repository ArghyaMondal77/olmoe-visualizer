THE DUAL-PATH ARCHITECTURE

THE RESIDUAL HIGHWAY

Deep neural networks suffer from vanishing gradients—as data passes through hundreds of layers, the signal gets corrupted or dies out.

OLMoE solves this by establishing a central "Spine". The raw data flows straight down this spine completely untouched. The Attention and MoE blocks merely read from the spine, compute new context, and add it back (+).

ATTENTION & ROPE

The Attention branch answers one question: "Which other words in this sentence matter to me?"

Before computing this, RoPE physically rotates the vectors based on their position in the sentence. Without RoPE, the model wouldn't know the difference between "The dog bit the man" and "The man bit the dog."

SPARSE EXPERT COMPUTE

A standard 7B model forces every token through all 7 Billion parameters. OLMoE uses Mixture of Experts (MoE).

The Router analyzes a token (like "Paris") and activates only 8 specialized sub-networks out of 64 (perhaps the geography and language experts). This means it performs like a 7B model, but runs as fast as a 1B model.
